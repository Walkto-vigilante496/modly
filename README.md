## 🖥️ How to Use modly

### Creating a 3D Model from an Image

1. Open modly and click the "From Image" button on the main screen.
2. Click "Upload" and select a photo from your computer. For best results, use a clear photo with a single object against a plain background.
3. Preview your image on the left side. Adjust settings like "Detail Level" (Low, Medium, High) — higher gives finer results but takes longer.
4. Click "Generate Model." A progress bar shows the AI working. This can take anywhere from 1 to 5 minutes depending on your GPU.
5. When done, the 3D model appears in the viewer. Use your mouse to drag and orbit around it, scroll to zoom, and right-click to pan.
6. Click "Export" to save your model as GLB, OBJ, or STL files — these work in Unity, Blender, 3D printers, and more.

### Creating a 3D Model from a Text Prompt

1. Click the "From Text" button on the main screen.
2. Type a detailed description in the text box. For example: "A vintage wooden pirate ship with three sails and a skull flag."
3. Choose a preset style (Realistic, Cartoon, Low-poly) if you want a specific look.
4. Click "Generate Model." Watch the AI craft your model layer by layer.
5. Review the result in the viewer. If you're not satisfied, tweak your description and try again.
6. Export your creation using the same "Export" button.

### Managing Your Models

Your generated models appear in a library called "My Models" on the home screen. You can organize them in folders, rename them, and delete ones you don't need. Each model lists details like file size, polygon count, and the date it was created.

## 🔧 Troubleshooting Common Issues

**The app is slow or crashes.**
Make sure your GPU drivers are up to date. Visit your graphics card manufacturer's website (NVIDIA, AMD, or Intel) and download the latest drivers. Also close other heavy programs like games or video editors while using modly.

**The model looks blurry or low-quality.**
Increase the "Detail Level" to High before generating. Also, make sure your source image is sharp and well-lit. For text prompts, add more adjectives to your description.

**"Out of Memory" error appears.**
Your GPU may not have enough VRAM. Try lowering the Detail Level, or close other GPU-using applications. If you have a lower-end GPU, stick to Low or Medium detail settings.

**The download page looks confusing.**
Look for a green button labeled "Latest Release" or a link with "Download" in it. If you see assets with "Source code" labels, scroll down to find the Windows installer file — it usually ends in `.exe` or `.msi`.

## 📚 Tips for Great Results

- Use high-resolution source images (at least 1000×1000 pixels) for photo-based generation.
- Describe objects with specific colors, materials, and shapes in text prompts.
- For best results, focus on one object per image or prompt — avoid busy scenes.

- If a model looks odd, try repeating the generation — AI can give different results each time.
